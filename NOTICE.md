## 1. Statutory Educational Tool Notice (Not Legal Advice)
Bill of Rights Bot is an educational statutory tool analyzing constitutional provisions, amendments, and governing case law. It does not provide legal advice or formal representation. Consult a licensed attorney or legal aid organization for legal matters.

## 2. Consumer Privacy Defaults & Air-Gap Invariant
By default, all operations execute 100% locally in volatile client memory with zero network transmission. Data is fully cleared on burn.
