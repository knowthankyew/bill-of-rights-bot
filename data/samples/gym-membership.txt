TITAN FITNESS CLUB MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT & TERMS OF SERVICE

1. MEMBERSHIP TERM & AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Your membership commences on the billing date and shall continue for an initial commitment period of twelve (12) consecutive months. Following the initial twelve-month term, this agreement automatically renews for successive one-year periods at the then-current rate unless cancelled in strict accordance with Section 4 below.

2. DUES, BILLING & UNILATERAL RATE ADJUSTMENTS
Monthly dues of $49.99 will be automatically deducted from your designated checking account or credit card on the 1st day of each month. Titan Fitness reserves the right to increase subscription fees and monthly dues at our sole discretion without prior notice.

3. FREE TRIAL & INTRODUCTORY PROMOTIONS
Any promotional free trial period will automatically convert into a paid full-price recurring subscription upon expiration. Unless cancelled prior to the end of the free trial, your card will be billed the full annual membership rate.

4. MANDATORY CANCELLATION PROCEDURE
To cancel your auto-renewing membership, you must call our central member services desk at 1-800-555-0199 Monday through Friday between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM EST, or send written notice via USPS certified mail with return receipt requested to Titan Member Services, PO Box 8492, Dallas, TX 75201. Cancellations are not accepted online, through the mobile application, or via email. Notice of cancellation must be submitted at least 60 days prior to the expiration of the current billing cycle.

5. NON-REFUNDABLE POLICY
All monthly charges, annual renewal fees, and prepaid amounts are final and non-refundable upon renewal under any circumstances, regardless of facility utilization.
