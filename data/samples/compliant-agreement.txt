OPENLAWN DIGITAL SUITE — TRANSPARENT SUBSCRIPTION TERMS

1. SUBSCRIPTION TERM & AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
OpenLawn Digital Suite operates on an annual subscription basis. Your membership will renew automatically on an annual basis at the clearly disclosed subscription rate of $120.00/year. 

2. ADVANCE RENEWAL REMINDER NOTICES
We will send you a reminder notice 15 to 45 days prior to each annual renewal date via email to your registered account. The reminder will specify the renewal date, the upcoming charge amount, and a direct link to cancel if you do not wish to continue.

3. IMMEDIATE CLICK-TO-CANCEL ONLINE MECHANISM
You can terminate online at will at any time. To cancel your subscription, simply click on the "Cancel Subscription" button in your account dashboard under Billing & Settings. Cancellation takes effect immediately at the conclusion of your current prepaid billing cycle, and no further charges will occur. We do not require you to call us or speak to a representative.

4. 14-DAY TRIAL TRANSPARENCY
If you enroll in a 14-day trial, we will remind you before your trial ends with an email 3 days prior to billing. You may cancel at any time during the trial in your account portal without charge.

5. PRICING CHANGES
Any future subscription rate changes will be communicated with at least 30 days advance notice via email, giving you the opportunity to cancel before the new rate applies.
